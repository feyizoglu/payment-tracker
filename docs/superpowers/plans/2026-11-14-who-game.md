# Who Am I? Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:test-driven-development for every task: write the failing test, watch it fail, implement, watch it pass, commit.

**Goal:** Add Who Am I? (TR Ben Kimim?), a forehead guessing game, to the Games section on web and mobile, offline like the other games.

**Architecture:** All rules live in the pure module `shared/who-game.ts`: setup defaults, validation (with the pool size passed in), `parseSavedWhoSetup` / `parseSavedWhoSeen`, pool and deck building from card sources passed in, and a reducer (`setup → handoff → turn → summary → end`) whose actions carry their time (`at`) and whose randomness comes from an injected `Rng`. It imports only `shared/who-cards/types.ts` and `shared/word-game-words/types.ts`, never a card list. `shared/who-things.ts` imports the six Describe It files the game uses (`{tr,en}/{animals,jobs,objects}.ts`). Only the game containers (web `WhoGame`, mobile `app/games/who.tsx`) import the card lists, statically, so they ship with the game's route and nothing else.

**Tech stack:** Next.js 16 client components with Tailwind v4, Expo SDK 57 with NativeWind 4 and expo-router, vitest in the node environment (components are called as functions or rendered through react-dom into a fake container).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-14-who-game-design.md`

## Decisions (the spec leaves them open)

1. **Setup shape:** `{ mode: "solo" | "teams", players, teams: [WordTeam, WordTeam], kinds, region: "tr" | "world" | "mixed", level: "easy" | "hard" | "mixed", turnSeconds, rounds }`. Both the solo list and the teams are kept, so switching the mode loses no names; validation looks only at the chosen mode's names.
2. **Defaults:** kinds People + Characters, region Mixed in TR and World in EN, level Mixed, 60 s, 2 rounds. `defaultWhoSetup(lang, teamNames)`; `parseSavedWhoSetup(raw, lang, teamNames)` falls back to it field by field.
3. **Validation:** solo 2–12 players (new `whoErrTooFew`, Imposter's strings for too many / blank / duplicate); teams use Describe It's team rules and strings (2–8 each, team names). Cards: `noKinds`, and `smallPool` when the pool has fewer than 50 cards (`smallPool` only when at least one kind is on). Rules: turn length and rounds out of range. The pool size is passed in, so the cards step, Start and "Start with last settings" all see the pool error.
4. **Pool:** People and characters filtered by region (Mixed = both) and level; things by level only. A pool card is `{ id, name, hint }` in the game's language, fixed at Start. Things keep their Describe It id with a `things:` prefix; their hint is the category's name (`imposterCategory{Animals,Jobs,Objects}`) in the game's language.
5. **Deck and seen:** as Describe It: unseen cards first (shuffled), then the seen ones; when fewer than 50 unseen remain, the pool's ids are dropped from the seen list. A card joins the seen list when it is shown. When the deck runs out mid-game it is refilled with the whole pool shuffled, the card just shown last, so a turn always has a card. The seen list is stored per language (`who_seen_<lang>`), written when a turn ends and at the end.
6. **Turn order:** solo: turn `n` belongs to player `n % players`, `players × rounds` turns. Teams: team `n % 2`, a team's `k`-th turn is guessed by `players[k % players.length]`, `2 × rounds` turns.
7. **Clock:** Hot Potato's model: `turnMs`, `usedMs`, `runningSince` (`null` while paused). On-screen time is `usedMs + (at - runningSince)`. `tick`, `pause` and `resume` skip the tap guard; `pause` / `resume` are idempotent; a tick while paused never ends the turn.
8. **Taps:** tap guard (400 ms) first, then the clock. A Got it / Pass at or after the turn end ends the turn instead of scoring, and the card on screen counts as passed; a tap swallowed by the guard changes nothing and the next tick ends the turn. The turn's end is recorded as the last action and as `endedAt`; flips and Next are refused for `WHO_SUMMARY_LOCK_MS` (1500) after it, and the summary shows its cards and Next disabled until then.
9. **Scores:** the turn's Got it count is added to the player or team on Next, so summary flips change the score; the summary shows the scores including this turn.
10. **End of turn feedback:** the summary screen mounts once per turn (`key` = turn number); on mount it vibrates once (web `navigator.vibrate` where it exists and does not throw; mobile `Vibration`) and announces "Time's up!" (web `role="alert"`, mobile `AccessibilityInfo`).
11. **Turn screen:** two large buttons fill the screen, Got it on top and Pass at the bottom (top and bottom do not swap for a holder facing away, left and right would), each labelled in text. The card (name, hint, time left) sits over the middle with pointer events off, so a tap there reaches the half below. The name's size comes from a pure `nameSize(name)` step by length (`xl`, `lg`, `md`, `sm`); mobile also uses `adjustsFontSizeToFit`.
12. **Teams step:** Describe It's `WordTeamsStep` is reused through an adapter that hands it a `WordSetup` built from the Who teams and takes back only `teams`; Describe It is not changed.
13. **Header during a turn:** `GameFrame` takes `cover` (web: the frame fills the viewport above the page header) and `GameScreen` takes `nav` (mobile: `nav={false}` leaves only the title). Both default to today's behaviour; only Who Am I? uses them: web covers the turn and the whole summary, mobile hides the header during the turn and until the summary's lock ends (the containers keep the `endedAt` whose lock has run out). Mobile also swallows Android back (`BackHandler`, return true) for that time while the screen is focused.
14. **Games list:** web lucide `UserRoundSearch` on `bg-emerald-50`, icon `text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400`, `hover:border-emerald-200` (add `--color-emerald-200` to `.dark` in `globals.css`); mobile 🤔 on `bg-emerald-50` (themed in `mobile/tailwind.css`).
